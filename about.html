THE IDEA BEHIND PERSIST

Build consistency. Track progress. Keep going.

PERSIST is a personal consistency and progress tracker designed to help turn everyday actions into lasting habits.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is PERSIST?

PERSIST is built around a simple idea: meaningful progress comes from showing up consistently.

The application allows users to create activities, complete their daily goals, build streaks, and keep track of their consistency over time.

Whether it is practicing DSA, completing assignments, working out, or developing another personal habit, PERSIST provides a simple way to stay accountable.

THE MOTIVATION

Why I Built PERSIST

Motivation can get you started, but consistency is what keeps you moving.

I wanted to build something that makes progress visible and encourages the habit of showing up every day.

PERSIST started as a project idea, but the goal behind it is much simpler: make small daily actions easier to track and turn them into meaningful progress.
